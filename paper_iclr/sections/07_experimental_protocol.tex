\section{Experimental protocol}

\paragraph{Experts and baselines.}
The V5 saved-output fraud pilot uses feature MLP, GCN, and GraphSAGE predictions for ten expert-training seeds. Its frozen primary family contains exactly four methods: uniform average, best fixed expert selected on source validation, a source-trained logistic gate, and \method{}. The primary offline comparator is a row-wise feasible hindsight oracle that includes the frozen-cost abstention action; the best fixed feasible non-abstaining expert is a secondary diagnostic. Neither can influence deployment. Broader benchmark phases are not silently added to the V5 pilot family.

\paragraph{Tuning and seeds.}
All deployable hyperparameters, normalization, diagnostics, checkpoints, and abstention thresholds use source train/validation only. The target protocol is absent from fitting. A target-unlabeled bundle contains predictions and permitted diagnostics but has no label field; the offline evaluator opens target labels only after a checksum-bound policy-freeze record exists. The policy freeze and every later output bind the effective execution hash, including chunk rows, real/synthetic mode, one-worker policy, code, dependency lock, and output/metric schemas. Seeds 1--10 index immutable expert predictions; router randomness is provenance, not an independent dataset replication.

\paragraph{Outcomes.}
Under \texttt{coregraph\_v5\_metric\_schema\_v2}, primary outcomes are the matched-action contract-regret metric, its maximum and CVaR summaries, \texttt{global\_target\_auprc}, recall at the recorded frozen review fraction, selective risk, coverage, routing stability, relative compute, and boolean resource feasibility. Its exact result-field name is \texttt{contract\_\allowbreak{}regret\_\allowbreak{}vs\_\allowbreak{}feasible\_\allowbreak{}row\_\allowbreak{}oracle}. Selective risk is not called selective AUPRC. The best-fixed quantities remain separately named diagnostics.

\paragraph{Statistical analysis.}
The paired unit is dataset, held-out target contract, and expert-prediction seed. Exact Wilcoxon and paired permutation tests, raw and robust standardized effects, and seed-block bootstrap intervals are computed separately per dataset. Holm correction applies within frozen claim families. A hierarchical dataset-seed bootstrap is secondary and cannot override a contradiction on one fraud dataset. Missing cells are not imputed.

\paragraph{Ablations and stress tests.}
Encoder ablations compare protocol one-hot, flat MLP, factorisation, bounded interactions, axis attention, uncertainty, discovery, and hybrid variants. Routing ablations compare contract, instance, and hierarchical modes. Diagnostic, regret/CVaR, budget, stability, uncertainty, sparsity, abstention, and mask terms are switched independently. Noisy categorical/continuous axes, delayed resource status, incorrect protocol labels, corrupted diagnostics, and out-of-support contracts are pre-registered.

\paragraph{Resource measurement and claim gates.}
The pilot cannot pass unless integrity/leakage gates are complete, maximum corrected regret improves directionally on both fraud datasets, no catastrophic primary reversal occurs, masks are exact, abstention has useful nonzero coverage, effects are not one-seed artifacts, and no oracle feature enters deployment. Packaging requires exact equality of planned and observed coordinate keys plus all identity and checksum fields before and after ZIP extraction.
