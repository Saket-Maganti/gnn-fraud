\section{Experimental design}

\paragraph{Surfaces.}
Fraud tasks comprise Elliptic and DGraphFin node prediction, Elliptic++ as a
correlated temporal corroboration, and IBM AML Small/Medium transaction-edge
prediction. T-Finance is included only if source, licence, and genuine
timestamps pass audit. General graph OOD uses three task-compatible GOOD node
settings. Controlled synthetic regimes vary feature and graph signal,
homophily, direction, drift, missing inputs, resource masks, and review budget.

\paragraph{Splits and access.}
Primary evaluation holds out unseen combinations of observed axis values.
Leave-one-axis-value, resource, budget, construction, visibility, temporal, and
random-environment splits are secondary controls. Domain generalisation,
unlabelled TTA, and few-label access are separate families.

\paragraph{Baselines and ablations.}
Baselines include individual experts, feasible averaging, source-validation
selection and convex mixing, the current graph-feature gate, atomic and
no-contract routers, the source-trained
\texttt{MOWST\_\allowbreak{}INSPIRED\_\allowbreak{}REIMPLEMENTATION}, and the
honestly named
\texttt{graphsafe\_\allowbreak{}confidence\_\allowbreak{}abstention\_\allowbreak{}component}.
The latter is not a full GraphSafe comparator: it remains a descriptive
compatibility component and is excluded from strong-baseline win requirements
and headline Holm families. Pending or unlicensed official code is excluded
rather than approximated under its name. For V5, a row-wise feasible hindsight
oracle over expert predictions plus the frozen-cost abstention action defines
the matched-action primary regret; the best fixed feasible non-abstaining
expert is diagnostic only.
Ablations remove factorised contracts, regret, budget, resource masks,
stability, abstention, or diagnostics.

\paragraph{Outcomes and inference.}
Primary outcomes include global target AUPRC, frozen-fraction recall,
budget-curve area, mean, maximum, and CVaR matched-action Brier regret, selective
zero--one risk, abstention cost, and compute. Binary-cross-entropy surrogate
regret is training terminology and does not enter the headline Brier family.
Unavailable and resource-blocked predictions do not enter
ranking metrics; fallback and abstention are distinct execution states.
Inference pairs exact task--target-protocol-and-contract--fold contexts within
dataset and expert-prediction seed. Same-numbered seeds across datasets are not
paired. Exact tests and intervals are reported separately per dataset; a
hierarchical dataset--seed bootstrap is secondary evidence only. The gate
requires positive effects on both datasets, corrected support on at least one,
and no contradiction on the other. Router-training seeds are deterministic
method-specific provenance, not additional replications. Exact Wilcoxon,
paired permutation, and seed-block bootstrap analyses replace normal
approximations. Holm-corrected support, confidence intervals, and frozen effect
thresholds affect the go/no-go decision. Worst-case improvement is the minimum
matched-contract difference within seed; maximum and CVaR regret are formed
from contract regret within seed.
