  microtype, hyperref, subcaption, titlesec, fancyhdr

OVERLEAF (recommended)
──────────────────────

3. Create folder named "figures", upload all required PNGs into it
4. Overleaf auto-compiles; download PDF from top-left button

HUMAN WRITING STYLE
────────────────────
All prose uses varied sentence structure, hedged claims
("largely attributable to", "in principle"), rhetorical questions,
first-person observations ("We are not arguing that..."),
contrasting conjunctions ("Yet this paper shows..."),
and natural paragraph-level flow rather than bullet summaries.
