\section{Conclusion}

CoReGraph treats graph deployment as a compositional contract rather than a
single split label. Its implementation makes graph visibility, target access,
expert feasibility, budget, compute, fallback, abstention, and evidence scope
machine-checkable. The checksum-gated V5 saved-output executor, target-label
firewall, atomic checkpoint protocol, and synthetic 240-coordinate campaign are
implemented and validated. The real pilot remains separately authorised and
unrun; empirical conclusions remain \pending.
