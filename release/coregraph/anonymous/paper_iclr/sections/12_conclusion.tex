\section{Conclusion}

We formulated graph deployment as compositional generalisation over typed contracts rather than selection under one protocol name. \method{} joins factorised uncertain metadata, expert diagnostics, hierarchical routing, exact resource masks, robust regret, and controlled abstention. The theory states why fixed mixtures can fail and when composition, masking, and selective transfer admit scoped guarantees. The benchmark and analysis plan are frozen across fraud, controlled mechanisms, and graph OOD.

The engineering and scholarly structure do not substitute for results. The six canonical saved-output archives and their 180 prediction members are locally checksum-verified. A checksum-gated V5 executor now materialises the frozen 60 scenarios and 540 bindings, enforces a target-label firewall, freezes each policy before offline evaluation, resumes from checksum-bound coordinates, and completes a deterministic 240-coordinate synthetic campaign. The real saved-output pilot has not run, official baselines are not installed, resources are unmeasured, and every numerical paper claim remains blocked. The next scientific step is a separately authorised real pilot using the frozen specification; no empirical success is concluded here.
