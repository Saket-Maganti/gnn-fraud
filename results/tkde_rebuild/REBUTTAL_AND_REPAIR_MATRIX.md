# Rebuttal and Repair Matrix

## Triage convention

- **FATAL_IF_MISREPRESENTED**: would invalidate a central claim if hidden or promoted.
- **MAJOR_ACCEPTANCE_RISK**: does not imply fabricated evidence, but can justify rejection at TKDE depth or generality standards.
- **NONFATAL**: should be repaired or disclosed before submission but does not invalidate the paper's bounded thesis.
- **REPAIRED**: fixed in the current manuscript and evidence pipeline.
- **REPAIRED_BY_SCOPE**: the underlying empirical limitation remains, but the claim is now stated at the supported scope.
- **OPEN_REPAIRABLE**: can be addressed from current text, code, or saved evidence without new training.
- **UNRESOLVED_EVIDENCE_LIMIT**: requires new data, runs, hardware, external users, or institutional validation.
- **RESOURCE_BLOCKED**: explicitly unmeasured under the recorded resource envelope.

## Rebuttal position by contribution

### Deployment contract

The defensible claim is that the six separately recorded, non-substitutable coordinates prevent temporal order, graph access, construction, selection, decision policy, and resources from being silently collapsed. The text and figure no longer call them statistically independent or exhaustive. K04 remains an external-validation limitation.

### Typed support relation

The support relation is valuable because it distinguishes incomplete scope, missing predictions, integrity failure, construct failure, resource failure, and directional contradiction. Its 14 fixtures validate implementation behavior. They do not prove scientific truth, ontology completeness, or curator agreement. That limitation is already stated and must remain.

### Empirical map

The real-data visibility results are the strongest controlled evidence. IBM construction and metric effects now use dependence-aware ten-seed inference and expose all 208 context-specific sensitivity rows; the resulting weaker NoEdge Small and GINE F1 conclusions are reported. Tasks remain separate, GINE remains Small-only, and Large remains unmeasured.

### Artifact

The artifact is strong at saved-output reconstruction and provenance, now covering 6,796 scalar records. The 14-page main PDF, 47-page supplement PDF, visual audit, curated archives, external checksums, and machine audit agree. A full clean-room training rerun is not claimed.
