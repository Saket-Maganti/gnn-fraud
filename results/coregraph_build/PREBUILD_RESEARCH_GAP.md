# Pre-build research gap

FraudShiftBench answers an evaluation-methodology question: deployment
protocols and graph construction can alter scientific conclusions. CoReGraph
must answer a different method question: how should a deployment system route
among heterogeneous graph, feature, temporal, and tree experts when the
contract is compositional and some experts are unavailable or too expensive?

The current repository does not yet support that claim for five reasons.

1. The deployment contract is prose-valued and not compositional. It cannot
   distinguish time, visibility, construction, selection, budget, and resource
   coordinates in a reusable validated API or construct unseen combinations.
2. Evidence and claim gates are dataset-specific. They cannot localise missing
   task units, resource boundaries, prediction alignment, pairing, statistical
   requirements, contradictions, or scope widening.
3. Evaluation remains node-centric and lacks leakage-safe fold-specific graph
   views. Edge/transaction fraud and target-structure access therefore cannot
   be compared honestly.
4. Existing GraphSafe and graph-feature gates are bounded wrappers. They do
   not provide factorised contract generalisation, multi-expert masks,
   worst-contract regret, CVaR, budget and compute objectives, abstention, or
   routing stability.
5. Strong direct competitors are not pinned or parity-audited, while the theory,
   experiment matrices, analysis plan, notebooks, anonymous release, and ICLR
   manuscript do not yet exist.

The new project will close these engineering and evaluation gaps without
renaming or regenerating FraudShiftBench. Scientific result claims remain
blocked until the declared later experiments are executed.

## Falsifiable method target

The later study may promote CoReGraph only if it improves the declared
worst-contract and regret outcomes against the strongest feasible baseline on
both real fraud datasets, preserves average utility, uses no target labels
outside the declared access regime, shows non-degenerate routing, and survives
the frozen paired analysis. Synthetic mechanism checks are necessary but are
not substitutes for real-data evidence.

## External gaps that code alone cannot resolve

- Official-baseline licences and API parity require upstream verification.
- Dataset licences, timestamps, and provider checksums require acquisition-time
  audit.
- T-Finance remains excluded from temporal claims unless genuine timestamps
  and source provenance pass.
- A project-wide licence cannot be inferred while
  `LICENSE_REVIEW_REQUIRED.md` remains unresolved.
- ICLR formatting and submission policy must be checked from official sources
  at submission time.
