# Research identity

CoReGraph asks whether a model can choose or combine feasible experts when the
deployment contract changes along six independently declared axes: time,
visibility, graph construction, selection access, decision budget, and
resources. ContractShiftBench is the evaluation layer that makes those shifts
typed, compositional, and auditable.

This is not a renaming of FraudShiftBench. The frozen TKDE work evaluates
protocol sensitivity. The new ICLR-track work studies contract-conditioned
routing, generalisation to unseen contract combinations, resource feasibility,
fallback, abstention, and worst-contract risk. Historical results are context,
not evidence for new empirical claims.

The primary unit of generalisation is a held-out contract combination. The
primary unit of prediction remains task-specific: node, edge, or transaction.
