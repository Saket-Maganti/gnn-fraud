# Level-4 terminology

- **Deployment contract**: typed time, visibility, construction, selection, budget, and resource coordinates governing admissible inference.
- **Base artifact**: immutable role-neutral prediction export for one dataset, protocol, expert, seed, and fold.
- **Scenario binding**: scenario-local assignment of a base artifact to source or target role with permitted row scope.
- **Unseen combination**: a tuple not present in source contracts whose individual axis values were source-observed.
- **Unseen axis value**: an axis value absent from source contracts; not covered by the additive theorem.
- **Feasible expert set**: experts satisfying availability, task, memory, latency, invocation, and policy constraints before routing.
- **Whole-contract feasible oracle**: one feasible expert selected after aggregating its risk over a contract; offline only.
- **Instance-clairvoyant oracle**: potentially different best expert per labelled instance; diagnostic ceiling only.
- **Contract router**: one expert distribution for a contract.
- **Instance router**: one expert distribution per instance.
- **Hierarchical router**: contract prior plus bounded instance correction.
- **Selective risk**: risk conditional on acceptance; undefined at zero coverage.
- **Composition generalisation gap**: matched seen-versus-unseen-combination performance difference.
- **Routing stability**: policy sensitivity to nearby contracts, metadata noise, score perturbations, and resource changes.
- **Resource blocked**: a cell that cannot be executed under the declared envelope; not a failed metric.
- **Results blocked**: method/protocol text exists but empirical evidence has not passed the claim gate.
