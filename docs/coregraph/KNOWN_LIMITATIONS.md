# Known limitations and honest blockers

- No provider dataset or heavy experiment has been executed in this build
  session; paper tables remain pending.
- Mowst, CIGA, TGN, and GOOD require isolated upstream installation and parity
  validation before official use.
- GraphMETRO and EERM lack a verified reusable licence at the pinned revision.
- CIGA is not task-equivalent to node/event fraud classification.
- IBM AML Large has no approved resource envelope.
- T-Finance has no valid temporal axis unless true timestamps are supplied.
- The target ICLR-year template and policy must be rechecked when published.
- Cost values are estimates until hardware profiling is complete.
- Theorems cover fixed-mixture and compositional/resource properties, not a
  universal guarantee that a learned router improves every contract.
