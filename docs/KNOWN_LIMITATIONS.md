# Known limitations

- Architecture breadth is narrower than a modern temporal or heterogeneous GNN
  survey.
- Predictive breadth covers two public real graphs and synthetic IBM AML
  regimes, not private-bank or cross-institution deployment.
- IBM visibility uses disclosed shared account-history covariates and does not
  support a pure first-50%-only interpretation.
- Ten seeds quantify optimization variation on fixed data/splits, not
  population variation across institutions or future periods.
- Several named cells remain resource-blocked or unmeasured; no performance
  direction is imputed.
- GraphSafe is a bounded saved-prediction case with mixed outcomes, not a
  universal method result.
- The support schema and 14 mutation cases validate declared implementation
  behavior, not ontology completeness, scientific truth, curator agreement,
  fairness, or operational safety.
- Saved-output reconstruction is deterministic, but a clean-machine full
  training rerun and external artifact badge are not claimed.
- T-Finance and other extension scaffolds must not be mistaken for measured
  final-manuscript evidence.
- Project-wide licensing and third-party redistribution review remain open.
